Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Hearthside is a homeschool record-keeping app for families. It is made for parents, and children use it only on devices a parent links. We collect only what the app needs to keep your family's records, we never sell anything, and we never show ads.

What we keep

  • The parent's email address, used to sign in with a one-time code.
  • Your family's records, as you enter them: children's first names and grades, school years, subjects and hours, courses and lessons, activities and outings, books, goals, learning plans, reports, and the work children do in the app.
  • Acellus course results, only if a parent chooses to sync them. The parent signs in to Acellus on Acellus's own page; Hearthside reads the course and grade information shown there.
  • Linked child devices. A child's device signs in without an email address and is linked to the family by a code the parent shows.

Where it's kept

Your records are stored with our database provider, Supabase, protected by sign-in and by rules that let a family see only its own records. Children's devices can see only what belongs to them.

Help from AI services

Some features (planning lessons, writing course material, rewriting a talking point for a child's reading level, pictures and read-aloud voices) use outside AI services: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI and ElevenLabs. We never send a child's name to these services. We send only what the task needs, such as a subject, a grade level or the text to rewrite. These services process the request for us and do not use it to identify your family.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone for their own use.
  • The camera is used only to scan a parent's link code. Nothing is recorded or kept.

Children

Hearthside is set up and controlled by a parent. A child's device works only after the parent links it, and children cannot create an account, add contacts, or share anything outside the family. A parent can see everything stored for their children.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your family's records for as long as your account is active. To delete your family and everything in it, email walfwood@me.com from the address you sign in with. We will delete it and confirm by reply.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
