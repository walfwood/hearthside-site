Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email walfwood@me.com. We read every message.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Enter your email address. Hearthside emails you a 6-digit code; there is no password.
  • How do I add a child's device? On the parent's phone, open the child's page and show the link code. On the child's iPhone or iPad, tap “Scan the code”.
  • How do I delete our family's data? Email us from the address you sign in with and we will delete your family and everything in it.
  • I'm a TestFlight tester. Thank you! Use the screenshot button in TestFlight to send feedback, or email us.
